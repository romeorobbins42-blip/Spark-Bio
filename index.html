WHO WE ARE

Advancing Science for the Public Good

Headquartered in San Diego, California, Spark Bio is an independent tax-exempt 501(c)(3) public charity organization (EIN: 85-1862988). We believe that biotechnology literacy and scientific research tools should be universally accessible to all communities without economic obstacles.

Our dedicated team collaborates with educators, students, and independent investigators to remove paywalls, curate peer-reviewed open educational resources, and support impactful community life science initiatives.

CORE PURPOSE

Our Mission & Vision

The guiding principles behind everything we do at Spark Bio.

🎯

Our Mission

To democratize access to biotechnology education and research resources, equipping students, educators, and independent investigators with the tools, knowledge, and collaborative networks necessary to solve critical global health and environmental challenges.

  • ▪ Removing cost barriers to scientific education
  • ▪ Empowering underserved school districts with lab resources
  • ▪ Championing open science methodologies
🔭

Our Vision

A world where geographic, economic, or institutional boundaries do not limit anyone's ability to participate in or benefit from life-saving biotechnology innovations and scientific discoveries.

  • ▪ Global scientific literacy by 2035
  • ▪ Inclusive networks for emerging researchers
  • ▪ Sustainable ecological biotech breakthroughs
